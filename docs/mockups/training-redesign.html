Training Page Redesign — 3 Approaches

Three different ways to organize ~95 hyperparameters. Each has tradeoffs noted at the top.

Approach A — Preset-First with Progressive Disclosure

Lead with a quick-start preset chooser. The form below auto-fills based on selected preset. Hide ~70% of fields by default behind an "Advanced" toggle. Best for: fast iteration, beginner-friendly, "I want to train a LoRA in 30 seconds." Trade-off: power users have to expand sections to see what they want.

Approach B — Tabbed Layout with Categorized Fields

Replace the accordion with horizontal tabs. Each tab is a self-contained section with its own search and reset button. Best for: power users who want everything organized cleanly and accessible in 1 click. Trade-off: takes vertical space for the tab bar; less progressive than approach A.

Approach C — Hybrid: Sticky Sidebar Navigation + Single Long Form

Single long scrollable form with a sticky left nav showing all sections. Click jumps to anchor. Fields stay grouped visually with visual badges (Essential / Advanced / Research). Best for: discoverability + power. Like Stripe's settings or Linear's preferences. Trade-off: more vertical scroll, but everything is "right there" without clicks.

Comparison Summary

A · Preset-First

Best for: speed
+ Fastest path to "click train"
+ Beginner-friendly
+ Right rail keeps command + start visible
− Power users need extra clicks
− Presets must be maintained

B · Tabbed

Best for: organization
+ Clean separation of concerns
+ Search bar for any field
+ Power users can pin a specific tab
− 6 tabs feels heavy at the top
− Loses sense of "scrolling through a config"

C · Sticky Nav + Long Form

Best for: power users
+ Everything visible on one scroll
+ Per-field badges (essential/advanced/research)
+ Linear / Stripe pattern — feels "real product"
− Lots of vertical scroll on small screens
− No quick start for beginners

My Recommendation

Hybrid of A and C. Take the preset chooser + right-rail summary/start from A, and the per-field badge system + sticky sub-nav from C. Skip B's tabs — they hide too much and force you to context-switch when reasoning about related settings (e.g. learning rate sits next to LR scheduler conceptually but they'd be in different tabs).

  1. Top of page: 4 preset cards (video, v2v, audio, av) + estimated time/VRAM card on the right.
  2. Left of form: Sticky sub-nav with sections grouped under Essentials, Advanced, Research.
  3. Form body: Long scrollable form with section anchors. Each field carries an inline badge (essential / advanced / research). Disabled features collapse their fields.
  4. Right of page: Persistent right rail with the Start button, command preview, and run summary (dataset + estimated time/VRAM).
  5. Inline warnings: If a field has a dangerous value, a small inline warning appears next to it (e.g., LR > 1e-3, max_steps < 100).
  6. Search: Keyboard shortcut / or Cmd+K opens a fuzzy field search. Quickly jump to any of the 95 fields.
